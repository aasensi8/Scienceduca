⚖️ Puzzle 1 · The mystery piece

The metal cube has an edge of 4 cm and the scale shows 0.1728 kg. Change the mass into grams, calculate the volume (cm³ and mL) and the density, and identify the metal.

🔢 Density in g/cm³ (2 decimals):

📖 Glossary
  • mass (massa) – how much matter an object has (g, kg)
  • volume (volum) – the space an object takes up (cm³, mL, L)
  • density (densitat) – mass divided by volume: d = m / V
  • edge (aresta) – the line where two faces of a cube meet
  • scale (balança) – a machine that measures mass
  • to round (arrodonir) – to make a number simpler: 2.666 → 2.67
💡 Hint 1One piece of data is in kg and you need grams.
💡 Hint 2Volume of a cube = edge × edge × edge.
💡 Hint 3Divide the mass in grams by the volume in cm³.

🪨 Puzzle 2 · The irregular stone

A stone of 67.5 g cannot be measured with a ruler. Look at how the water in the cylinder goes up when you drop the stone in.

🔢 Volume of the stone in cm³ (2 decimals):

Also calculate the density (2 decimals). Would it float in water? 🤔

📖 Glossary
  • water displacement (desplaçament d’aigua) – a way to find the volume of an irregular object
  • graduated cylinder (probeta) – a tall glass tube with a scale in mL
  • level (nivell) – how high the water is
  • initial / final (inicial / final) – at the start / at the end
  • to float (flotar) – to stay on the surface of a liquid
  • to sink (enfonsar-se) – to go down to the bottom
💡 Hint 1The level goes up because the stone takes up space.
💡 Hint 2Volume = final level − initial level, and 1 mL = 1 cm³.
💡 Hint 3Density = 67.5 g ÷ volume. Compare it with 1 g/cm³.

🚀 Extensions

✅ Answer key

P1. 0.1728 kg = 172.8 g. V = 4³ = 64 cm³ = 64 mL. d = 172.8 ÷ 64 = 2.70 g/cm³ → aluminium (2700 kg/m³).

P2. V = 85 − 60 = 25.00 mL = 25.00 cm³. d = 67.5 ÷ 25 = 2.70 g/cm³ > 1: it sinks.

P3. A: 0.92; B: 1.00; C: 1.26; D: 0.80 g/mL. From the bottom to the top: C, B, A, D (CBAD).

P4. Wood: 200 cm³ → 0.60 (floats). Iron: 7.80 (sinks). Cork: 6/25 = 0.24 (floats). Ice: 27 cm³ → 0.90 (floats). Clay: 150 g / 100 cm³ = 1.50 (sinks). 3 objects float.

P5. Dials: 3 · 2 · 5 · 7 → 3257. Check: 2 dm³ = 0.002 m³; m = 2700 × 0.002 = 5.40 kg.

Extensions. 1) V = 20 cm³; m = 8.96 × 20 = 179.20 g. 2) d = 78/40 = 1.95 g/cm³ > 1: it sinks. 3) 8.96 g/cm³ = 8960 kg/m³.
